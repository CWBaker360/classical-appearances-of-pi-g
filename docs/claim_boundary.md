# Claim Boundary

The substitutions in the paper are not new analytic identities. They follow
from the established equality

\[
\pi_g=\pi.
\]

The finite numerical demonstration uses a 64-bit approximant
\(\pi_g^{(N)}\), Simpson quadrature on \([-6,6]\), and ordinary floating-point
arithmetic. It illustrates consistency but does not prove the Gaussian
integral or independently establish \(\pi_g=\pi\).
